Harbor Field Notes

The morning ferry crosses the bay before the streets become busy. From the deck, passengers can watch the shoreline change from a row of houses to a sheltered stretch of forest. This route has connected the communities for generations, carrying schoolchildren, workers, and visitors through every season.

At the harbor, a small workshop maintains the wooden boats used by local crews. The builders measure each plank carefully and explain how the curve helps a boat move through rough water. Their notes document years of repairs and preserve practical knowledge for the next group of apprentices.

Walking beyond the harbor reveals a public trail that follows the water. Benches offer places to pause, while signs describe the plants that grow along the coast. Residents care for the path together, repairing steps after storms and keeping the shoreline accessible throughout the year.
